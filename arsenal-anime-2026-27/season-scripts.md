# 砲兵 GUNNERS: 王冠を守れ — *Defend the Crown*
### An animated Arsenal journey through the 2026/27 season

**Status:** scripts only. Nothing has been generated on Higgsfield.
**Scope:** a prologue (the 2026 Champions League final) plus every competitive match up to the September international break. New episodes get added as the season goes on.
**Runtime:** each episode is 42–52 seconds, written as 4–8 second beats so each beat can map to one video clip later.

---

## The story

Arsenal are **champions of England**, and they are also **the team that lost in Budapest**.

On 30 May 2026 they went ahead in the Champions League final after six minutes and took PSG to extra time and penalties, then lost the shootout 4–3. Eze missed. Raya saved one. Then Gabriel, the defender who had carried them all season, put the deciding kick over the bar.

The series follows the clan through one season with two goals:

1. **Defend the crown.** Keep the Premier League trophy in North London.
2. **Get past Budapest.** Deal with the memory of that night and get back to a Champions League final, this time in **Madrid (Estadio Metropolitano, 5 June 2027)**.

### Story threads that run across the season

| Thread | Arc |
|---|---|
| **Gabriel's ghost** | He keeps seeing the ball flying over the bar. Every clean sheet and every header he wins is him fighting back. His return to the Champions League in Naples (EP5) is the emotional turning point. |
| **Eze's silence** | He also missed in the shootout. He doesn't talk about it; he answers it on the pitch. His assist for Dowman (EP7) is his first bright moment. |
| **Havertz's goal "that wasn't enough"** | He scored in the final. This season, every goal he scores is a promise that the next one will count. |
| **Raya, the guardian** | He saved a kick in Budapest. His penalty save at Sunderland (EP6) shows the clan that spot kicks can go their way. |
| **Arteta's fire** | The sensei refuses to let the club hide from Budapest. His mantra: *"We don't run from the pain. We use it."* |
| **The crown** | The Premier League trophy stands in the Cannon Castle's shrine room. The scoreboard at the end of each Premier League episode shows how the title defence is going. |

### Visual language
- The Emirates is drawn as the **Cannon Castle** (大砲城), with the Premier League trophy kept in a shrine room. Arsenal wear red-and-white haori over the kit; opponents are rival clans in their club colours.
- **Budapest flashbacks** are black-and-white ink with a single red element: the ball.
- Goals: freeze-frame, ink splash, 「ゴール！」 in brushed katakana.
- Palette: Arsenal red #EF0107, white, gold. PSG is only ever shown as a shadowy dark-blue silhouette.

## Before generating on Higgsfield (when you're ready)

- **Handle the Budapest scenes with care.** Gabriel and Eze are real people, and a real miss is a sore subject. The script makes them heroes of their own comeback, never figures of mockery. Keep it that way when writing prompts.
- **Copyright.** Don't have a model generate *The Angel (North London Forever)* or the Champions League anthem. License them or use the stand-in or original music.
- **Real people's likenesses.** Models often refuse photoreal faces of real people, and publishing them carries personality-rights risk. Use stylised anime characters identified by name, squad number and hairstyle.
- **Club and competition marks.** The Arsenal, PSG, Premier League and UEFA crests, logos and trophies are trademarks. Use the invented cannon-castle crest, a generic "crown" trophy and a generic star-ball.
