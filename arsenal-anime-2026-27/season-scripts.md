# 砲兵 GUNNERS — Arsenal 2026/27 Anime Series (Scripts)

**Status:** scripts only. Nothing has been generated on Higgsfield.
**Scope:** every competitive first-team match from the start of the season to the September international break (8 matches). New episodes get added as more matches are played.
**Runtime:** each episode is ~40 seconds (minimum 30), written as 5-second beats so each beat can map to one video clip later.

---

## Series concept

Arsenal is a samurai clan, the **Gunners of the Cannon Castle** (大砲城, *Taihō-jō*): the Emirates drawn as a Japanese castle with a golden cannon on the roof. Every match is a battle. Every episode has the same shape:

1. **Cold open.** Kanji title card, brush stroke, taiko hit.
2. **Locker room (控室, *hikaeshitsu*).** A short scene before or after the match. *These scenes are made up and are not reports of anything that really happened.*
3. **The battle.** The real scoreline, scorers and key moments, drawn as anime (speed lines, glowing boots, cherry blossom bursting on each goal).
4. **Scoreboard card.** The exact final score in both kanji and numerals.

**Visual language**
- Arsenal: red and white haori over the kit, a cannon crest glowing on the chest. Opponents are shown as rival clans in their club colours.
- Goals: freeze-frame, ink splash, then 「ゴール！」 in huge brushed katakana.
- Mikel Arteta is the clan's **sensei**, in a dark hakama with a tactics scroll.
- Palette: Arsenal red #EF0107, white, gold; night matches get lantern-lit skies.

**Recurring locker room bits** (all fictional):
- Rice keeps the kettle going and makes green tea for everyone.
- Ødegaard (captain) gives each pre-match speech in one calm line.
- Saka and Lewis-Skelly have a running game of rock-paper-scissors.
- Raya polishes his gloves like a katana before every match.
- Sensei Arteta's whiteboard is a folding shoji screen.

## Fact-check notes

Sources: ESPN, Sky Sports, NBC Sports, UEFA, Al Jazeera, VAVEL and Arseblog match reports, found by web search. I could only see search summaries, not the full pages, because this environment blocks those sites. **Check minutes and assists against an official match report before anything is generated.** Unconfirmed items:
- **EP1:** minutes of Havertz's and Ødegaard's goals.
- **EP4:** minute of Havertz's equaliser ("midway through the first half").
- **EP6:** the penalty was saved about 11 minutes after the restart (~56'). One source dated this match 20 Sep; ESPN, Bleacher Report and the Premier League all say **12 Sep**.
- **EP7:** minutes of the Madueke, Merino, Mehmeti and Akpom goals.
- All **locker room scenes are invented** for the story and don't claim to show real events.

## Before generating on Higgsfield (when you're ready)

- **Real people's likenesses.** Every player and Arteta is a real person. Image and video models often refuse photoreal likenesses or recognisable faces of real people, and publishing them carries personality-rights risk. The safer route is stylised anime characters identified by name, squad number and hairstyle, not a face match.
- **Club and competition marks.** The Arsenal crest, Premier League and Champions League logos, and sponsor logos are trademarks. Use the invented cannon-castle crest and generic trophies in the visuals. Fine for a fan project; get permission before any commercial use.
