# 砲兵 GUNNERS — Arsenal 2026/27 Anime Series (Scripts)

**Status:** scripts only. Nothing has been generated on Higgsfield.
**Scope:** every competitive first-team match from the start of the season to the September international break (8 matches). New episodes get added as more matches are played.
**Runtime:** each episode is 42–52 seconds (minimum 30), written as 4–8 second beats so each beat can map to one video clip later.

---

## Series concept

Arsenal is a samurai clan, the **Gunners of the Cannon Castle** (大砲城, *Taihō-jō*): the Emirates drawn as a Japanese castle with a golden cannon on the roof. Every match is a battle. Every episode has the same shape:

1. **Cold open.** Kanji title card, brush stroke, taiko hit.
2. **Locker room (控室, *hikaeshitsu*).** A short scene before, during or after the match. *These scenes are made up and are not reports of anything that really happened.*
3. **The battle.** The real scoreline, scorers and key moments, drawn as anime.
4. **The terraces (応援団, *ōendan*).** The fans sing in English. They are the Gooner *ōendan*, the cheer squad from Japanese school sport, waving giant flags and pounding taiko drums.
5. **Scoreboard card.** The exact final score in both kanji and numerals.

**Visual language**
- Arsenal: red and white haori over the kit, a cannon crest glowing on the chest. Opponents are shown as rival clans in their club colours.
- Goals: freeze-frame, ink splash, then 「ゴール！」 in huge brushed katakana.
- Palette: Arsenal red #EF0107, white, gold; night matches get lantern-lit skies.

### The anthem — "The Angel (North London Forever)"

*The Angel (North London Forever)* by Louis Dunford is the song the Emirates crowd sings as the teams walk out. The script places it at **both home games (EP2, EP4)**: 60,000 fans hold their scarves up and sing. **The lyrics are not written out here, and the song must not be recreated with AI.** It's a copyrighted recording. In the edit, either license the real track or use the original stand-in below.

**Original stand-in anthem, "Cannon Castle Forever"** (use this if the real song isn't licensed):
> *"Red lanterns glow on Holloway Road, / our fathers sang, now the song is ours, / through rain and loss we carry the load — / Cannon Castle, forever ours."*

## Fact-check notes

Sources: ESPN, Sky Sports, NBC Sports, UEFA, Al Jazeera, VAVEL and Arseblog match reports, found by web search. I could only see search summaries, not the full pages, because this environment blocks those sites. **Check minutes and assists against an official match report before anything is generated.** Unconfirmed items:
- **EP1:** minutes of Havertz's and Ødegaard's goals. Havertz's goal is shown at the back post for drama; how it was actually scored isn't confirmed.
- **EP4:** minute of Havertz's equaliser ("midway through the first half"). The header is drawn for drama; how it was actually scored isn't confirmed.
- **EP6:** the penalty was saved about 11 minutes after the restart (~56'). ESPN, Bleacher Report and the Premier League date this match **12 Sep**.
- **EP7:** minutes of the Madueke, Merino, Mehmeti and Akpom goals.
- All **locker room scenes, dialogue, touchline lines and chants are invented** for the story. The one exception is Arteta calling the Brighton defeat a "big lesson", which he said in real post-match interviews.
