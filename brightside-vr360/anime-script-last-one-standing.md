# 最後の一人 SAIGO NO HITORI: "LAST ONE STANDING"
### Episode 1: "Cull Protocol" (カル・プロトコル)
*An original anime short about espionage, assassins and military combat. Runtime about 5:00, 16:9. Written for a faceless YouTube channel.*

> **About the inspiration:** I couldn't confirm which Japanese series "The Last Man Standing" you mean. This is an **original** story in that spirit: a small group of people with powers, a hidden program, and a rule that only one of them walks away. None of the characters, names or plot come from an existing show, so it's safe to publish.

---

## 1. Premise

In 2031, Japan's secret defence program **PROJECT KAGE (影 "Shadow")** creates six **"Gifted"** operatives, each with one ability that has a painful cost. Four of them work together as **Unit Ghost-4**. Their handler sends them to an abandoned naval fortress to rescue a defector.

On the defector's data drive is the **CULL PROTOCOL**: the program will pull out **only one Gifted**, and whoever survives the mission is the one who gets out. Their helicopter has **one seat**.

**Series hook:** every episode, the team is pushed to turn on each other, and every episode they refuse. *"Then we'll all be the last one standing."*

**Tone:** gritty spy thriller mixed with stylish super-power action. Think tactical gear, rain, neon, and slow-motion fights.
**Content level:** YouTube-safe. Stylised action, no blood or gore, and injuries are shown as impact flashes, sparks and shattered armour.

## 3. Visual & production bible

- **Style lock (paste into every Seedance prompt):** *"Japanese TV anime, high-budget action anime style, cel-shaded 2D with cinematic lighting, dramatic speed lines, rain and neon reflections, teal-and-crimson palette, sharp lineart, no on-screen text, no blood or gore."*
- **Colours:** night teal, warning crimson, sodium orange. Each ability has its own glow:
  - Sansei: amber afterimages
  - Heat Haze: shimmering heat distortion
  - Iron Skin: a gunmetal ripple across the skin
  - Echo: cyan sound-wave rings
- **Camera language:** dutch angles at tense moments, 180° whip-pans between characters, an impact frame (a flash of inverted black and white) on every big hit, slow-motion ramps when abilities fire.
- **Location:** **Tsurugi Island Fortress** 剣島, a decommissioned WWII-era naval fortress now used as a black-site. Concrete bunkers, rusted artillery, radar dishes, a helipad in the rain.
- **Audio:** taiko drums mixed with synth for action; a heartbeat that muffles into silence during Echo; the Japanese title shouted over the drop. Voices in Japanese with English subtitles (or an English dub) via Higgsfield text-to-speech.
- **Consistency:** make one character sheet per character first, then pass them into every Seedance 2.0 clip as `image_references`.

## 4. Script

*Format: scene heading, then **ACTION**, **DIALOGUE** (Japanese line with the English subtitle underneath), and SFX/MUSIC. Times are approximate.*

### COLD OPEN (0:00–0:20)

**EXT. TSURUGI ISLAND HELIPAD — NIGHT — RAIN**
Extreme close-up: **JIN's** amber eye snaps open. Rapid **amber afterimage flashes**:
- Rei falls, flickering out of invisibility.
- Daigo's steel skin cracks.
- Yume's headphones hit the wet concrete.
- A faceless white mask steps through the smoke.

Cut back: Jin gasps, one knee on the ground, rain hissing around him. The helicopter's rotors spin up behind him.

> **JIN** *(whisper)*: 三秒後…全員死ぬ。
> *"In three seconds… every one of us is dead."*

**SFX:** heartbeat, heartbeat, then **silence**.
**SMASH CUT** to the title: **最後の一人 LAST ONE STANDING** *(taiko hit)*

### ACT 1: INFILTRATION (0:20–1:15)

**EXT. SKY ABOVE THE PACIFIC — NIGHT — 20 MINUTES EARLIER** *(caption in the edit)*
A HALO jump. Four silhouettes fall through storm clouds, and lightning shows the fortress below.

> **KUROGANE** *(comms, calm)*: ゴースト・フォー。目標は藤森博士。生きたまま回収しろ。
> *"Ghost-4. Target: Dr. Fujimori. Retrieve her alive."*

**EXT. FORTRESS CLIFFS — CONTINUOUS**
They land on the cliffs. **YUME** kneels, pushes her headphones on, and **cyan sound rings** ripple out across the island.

> **YUME**: 心音、四十二。機械音…多すぎる。無人機がいる。
> *"Forty-two heartbeats. Too much machinery… there are drones."*

**EXT. FORTRESS GATE — CONTINUOUS**
Two guards under a floodlight. **REI** takes a breath and **shimmers out of sight** (heat-haze distortion). An **underwater-style muffled audio** shift for her POV. The guards drop one after the other with silenced shots and a wire. She reappears, gasping.

> **REI**: 門、クリア。
> *"Gate's clear."*

**DAIGO** walks up, cracking his neck.

> **DAIGO**: 静かすぎて退屈だな。
> *"Too quiet. I'm bored."*

> **JIN**: すぐ退屈じゃなくなる。
> *"You won't be for long."*

### ACT 2: FIREFIGHT (1:15–2:30)

**INT. FORTRESS CORRIDOR — NIGHT**
An alarm blares and red lights start spinning. **Armoured soldiers** and **quad-legged combat drones** pour in from both ends.

**DAIGO** steps forward and his skin ripples to **gunmetal steel**. Bullets spark off him in slow motion while he raises the mini-gun.

> **DAIGO**: 鉄皮！ 隠れてろ、ガキども！
> *"IRON SKIN! Get behind me, kids!"*

**JIN** fights at close quarters through the smoke. His **amber afterimages** show him every attack three seconds early, and he slips past blades and bullets like a dance. Impact frames flash on each hit.

**EXT. FORTRESS ROOFTOP — CONTINUOUS**
**YUME** lies prone with her sniper rifle and fires through a radar dish at a drone's core. Each shot fires as a cyan ring pulses.

> **YUME**: ジン、左から三体！ …違う、四体！
> *"Jin, three from the left! …No, four!"*

**JIN** *(already moving; he saw it coming)*: 知ってる。
*"I know."*

**INT. LAB CORRIDOR — CONTINUOUS**
**REI**, invisible, runs past a line of soldiers who never see her and **slides under a closing blast door**. She comes up gasping on the other side, face to face with…

### ACT 3: THE ONE SEAT (2:30–3:40)

**INT. UNDERGROUND LAB — NIGHT**
**DR. AYA FUJIMORI** (40s, lab coat, cracked glasses) clutches a glowing data drive. Monitors behind her show six silhouettes labelled **GIFTED 01–06**.

> **FUJIMORI**: あなたたち、何も知らされてないのね。
> *"They didn't tell you anything, did they."*

The team gathers. She plugs in the drive and a red file opens: **CULL PROTOCOL** (show it as an icon on the monitor, and add the text in the edit).

> **FUJIMORI**: プロジェクト影は終わる。回収されるのは…ギフテッド一人だけ。
> *"Project Kage is being shut down. Only one Gifted gets extracted."*

The team freezes. Each of them glances, just for a moment, at the others' hands and weapons.

> **KUROGANE** *(comms, cold)*: 聞いた通りだ。ヘリの席は一つ。最後に立っていた者が乗る。
> *"You heard her. One seat on the helicopter. The last one standing gets on."*

A long, tense silence. Rain drips. **REI** puts her hand on her pistol.

> **DAIGO** *(laughs, and his steel skin fades)*: くだらねえ。
> *"What a load of crap."*

> **JIN**: ギフテッドは六人。俺たちは四人。…残りの二人はどこだ？
> *"There are six Gifted. We're four. So where are the other two?"*

The lights go out. In the dark, a **smooth white mask** tilts its head.

### ACT 4: HOLLOW (3:40–4:40)

**INT. UNDERGROUND LAB — CONTINUOUS**
**HOLLOW** blurs forward and **touches DAIGO's arm**. The steel ripple **spreads onto HOLLOW**. He punches, and Daigo crashes through a wall.

> **HOLLOW** *(soft, echoing)*: 君の力、借りるよ。
> *"I'll borrow your power."*

**YUME** shouts a warning, but HOLLOW touches her next. **Cyan rings** burst from him and he now hears everything. He blocks Jin's first attack without looking.

**JIN** activates **Sansei** again and again. His eye **bleeds amber light** and the afterimages start to **glitch**.

> **JIN** *(through gritted teeth)*: あと…二回が限界だ。
> *"Two more… that's my limit."*

**THE PLAN:** a rapid montage cut with whip-pans.
1. **YUME** takes off her headphones and **screams into a comms amplifier**. The feedback overloads HOLLOW's **borrowed Echo**, and he staggers with his hands on his ears.
2. **DAIGO** bursts out of the rubble, now back in iron, and **pins HOLLOW** to the floor.
3. **REI** holds her breath and **vanishes**. HOLLOW, deafened, can't find her.
4. **JIN** uses his **last Sansei**. The amber afterimage shows exactly where HOLLOW will be in three seconds.

> **JIN**: レイ。三秒後、右。
> *"Rei. Three seconds. Right."*

The 3-second countdown freeze-frames. **REI reappears** in mid-air, and her strike shatters **HOLLOW's mask** in an impact frame. We never see his face: a white flash, then he's gone in a burst of mist (朝霧 means "morning mist").

**EXT. HELIPAD — MOMENTS LATER — DAWN BREAKING**
The four limp out with Fujimori. The one-seat helicopter waits, its rotors turning.

> **KUROGANE** *(comms)*: 決めろ。誰が乗る？
> *"Decide. Who gets on?"*

The team looks at each other. **DAIGO** lifts the pilot out of the seat with one arm and politely sets him down on the helipad.

> **DAIGO**: 全員乗る。定員オーバーだけどな。
> *"All of us. Bit over capacity, though."*

> **JIN** *(to the comms)*: 最後に立ってるのは…俺たち全員だ。
> *"The last one standing… is all of us."*

Jin **crushes the earpiece**. The crowded helicopter lifts off into the sunrise. **Hero shot** of all four hanging off the skids, backlit by the dawn.

### STINGER (4:40–5:00)

**INT. KAGE COMMAND — DARK ROOM**
A hand with a **military prosthetic** turns off a monitor showing the helicopter. We see **COMMANDER KUROGANE** for the first time: half his face scarred, and one eye glowing **the same amber as Jin's**.

> **KUROGANE**: 想定通りだ。フェーズ２を開始しろ。…六人目を起こせ。
> *"As expected. Begin Phase Two… wake the sixth."*

A cryo-pod hisses open, and a **pale hand** grips its edge.

**END CARD:** *"EPISODE 2 — at 10K likes?"* with the title logo and subscribe button.
