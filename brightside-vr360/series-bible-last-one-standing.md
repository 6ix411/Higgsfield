# 最後の一人 LAST ONE STANDING: Series Bible (Season 1)
**Format:** 20 episodes × about 5:00 (20 Seedance clips of 15 s each per episode), 16:9, Japanese voices with English subtitles. Made for a faceless YouTube channel.
**Genre:** espionage, assassins and military combat, with a few people who have special powers. **Every episode has one betrayal or twist and ends on a cliffhanger.**
Episode 1's full script is in `anime-script-last-one-standing.md`.

---

## 1. Series spine

PROJECT KAGE made six "Gifted" soldiers with powers. Its **Cull Protocol** says only one of them survives. Over the season the team learns:
1. that the people who handle them are lying,
2. that someone on their own team always is too, and
3. that the Gifted were all built from a **single original**: Commander Kurogane himself.

The final question is whether "the last one standing" is a person or a choice.

**Rules for every episode**
- **Cold open (0:00–0:20):** a flash-forward, a threat, or a replay of an earlier scene that now means something different.
- **One betrayal or twist per episode,** hinted at on screen before it happens so people who rewatch can spot it.
- **One showcase of a power,** each with its own glow: amber = Sansei, heat-haze = invisibility, gunmetal = Iron Skin, cyan = Echo, mist = Mimic.
- **Ending (4:40–5:00):** a cliffhanger plus "Episode N+1 at X likes".
- **YouTube-safe:** stylised action with no blood or gore.

## 3. Episode guide

### ARC 1: CULL 淘汰 (Episodes 1–5)
**Ep. 1 "Cull Protocol" (カル・プロトコル)**
- **Story:** the rescue at Tsurugi Island. The defector turns out to be bait, HOLLOW is Daigo's brother, and there's a one-seat helicopter.
- **Twist:** Rei injects Daigo to take away his power, then double-crosses the handler and leaves alone.
- **Ends on:** the Jin clone wakes up. *(Full script written.)*

**Ep. 2 "Stranded" (漂流)**
- **Story:** the self-destruct countdown on the island. The three survivors escape, damaged: Jin is blind, Daigo powerless, Yume half-deaf. Smuggler **Captain Mako** pulls them out of the sea, for a price.
- **Twist:** Yume secretly gets a coded message from Rei ("Don't trust the next voice you hear") and hides it from the others.
- **Ends on:** Mako's radio crackles with **Kurogane's voice** negotiating a sale.

**Ep. 3 "Two Foxes" (二匹の狐)**
- **Story:** ROKU attacks the smuggler ship during a storm. Jin's sight is only half back, and ROKU sees twice as far into the future. Jin loses badly.
- **Twist:** ROKU spares him: "Kurogane said never to damage the *original*." The team is left wondering who the original is.
- **Ends on:** ROKU leaves a photo of a young Jin with **Kurogane's hand on his shoulder**.

**Ep. 4 "Broken Mirror" (割れた鏡)**
- **Story:** a raid on a KAGE memory lab in Kobe to learn the truth about Jin. Daigo's steel skin comes back mid-fight.
- **Twist:** the files show that the whole team's childhood memories were **implanted**. They were never orphans, just raised in tanks.
- **Ends on:** Dr. Fujimori is in the lab, and she **surrenders to them**.

**Ep. 5 "Heartbeat" (心音)** *(end of Arc 1)*
- **Story:** the team interrogates Fujimori in a flooded subway station. KAGE troopers attack, and Rei **comes back** mid-battle to fight beside them.
- **Twist:** Fujimori switches sides for real. Yume confirms her heartbeat is honest this time, and she says: *"Kurogane isn't culling you. He's choosing a body."*
- **Ends on:** Rei's earpiece lights up. She is still reporting to Kurogane.

### ARC 2: HUNTED 狩り (Episodes 6–10)
**Ep. 6 "Double Agent" (二重スパイ)**
- **Story:** told from Rei's point of view. We see what she really did in Ep. 1 and why: KAGE holds her little sister **Sora**. A stealth mission inside KAGE HQ.
- **Twist:** Kurogane knew about her double-cross all along. It was part of his plan.
- **Ends on:** Kurogane gives Rei a new order: *"Bring me Daigo's brother."*

**Ep. 7 "Neon Tokyo" (ネオン東京)**
- **Story:** a chase across Shinjuku rooftops in the rain. The team tracks HOLLOW/Kenji, who is on the run from KAGE, his memories breaking down.
- **Twist:** Kenji chose to join KAGE after his "death" to protect Daigo, and gave up his own memories to do it.
- **Ends on:** Daigo shields Kenji from a sniper. **The sniper is Yume's rifle**, but Yume is asleep in the safehouse.

**Ep. 8 "The Price" (代償)**
- **Story:** a heist at Mako's floating black market. The team trades Fujimori's research for a way into KAGE's servers.
- **Twist:** Mako sells them to KAGE for a pardon, and the market becomes a trap full of drones.
- **Ends on:** Yume hears **one extra heartbeat in the team's own van**.

**Ep. 9 "Inside Voice" (内なる声)**
- **Story:** a hunt for the mole inside the team. Suspicion falls on Rei and fists fly.
- **Twist:** the mole is **Yume, without knowing it**. Her Echo implant has been transmitting everything to Kurogane since Ep. 1, including the "sniper shot" in Ep. 7, which was fired remotely.
- **Ends on:** Yume begs them to cut the implant out. Without it, she loses her power.

**Ep. 10 "Brother's Keeper" (兄の番人)** *(midseason finale)*
- **Story:** the Kenji swap. Kurogane offers to cure Kenji's memory loss in exchange for one Gifted.
- **Twist:** **Daigo betrays the team.** He knocks out Jin and hands **Yume** to KAGE to save his brother.
- **Ends on:** Kenji, now cured, sees what Daigo did and says: *"I'd rather have stayed broken."*

### ARC 3: MIRRORS 鏡 (Episodes 11–15)
**Ep. 11 "Without Sound" (無音)**
- **Story:** Yume is held in KAGE's anechoic prison. She escapes deafened by using the drones she can still feel through vibration.
- **Twist:** the only person who helps her is **ROKU**, who is curious about emotions.
- **Ends on:** ROKU asks, "What does betrayal feel like?" and Yume answers, "Ask Daigo."

**Ep. 12 "The Sister" (妹)**
- **Story:** Rei and Jin rescue **Sora** from a KAGE children's facility in the snowy mountains of Hokkaido.
- **Twist:** Sora doesn't want to be rescued. She calls Kurogane "father."
- **Ends on:** an avalanche. Rei has to choose between saving Sora and saving Jin.

**Ep. 13 "Iron and Mist" (鉄と霧)**
- **Story:** Daigo, full of guilt, versus Kenji, back as HOLLOW, fighting in a shipyard. Steel against mist.
- **Twist:** Kenji copies Iron Skin *and* Daigo's heart condition. Neither brother can win without killing the other, so both stop.
- **Ends on:** Daigo turns himself in to the team: "Do what you want with me."

**Ep. 14 "Trial" (裁き)**
- **Story:** the team, reunited but broken, puts Daigo on trial in an abandoned temple. Yume returns.
- **Twist:** Yume **forgives** him, then reveals she kept a sliver of the implant on purpose, so the team can feed Kurogane false information.
- **Ends on:** a false plan is sent. Kurogane smiles: "The girl thinks I'm still listening."

**Ep. 15 "The Minister" (大臣)**
- **Story:** the team infiltrates a gala in Tokyo Tower to reach **Minister Saionji**, who funds KAGE.
- **Twist:** Saionji wants Kurogane gone too, and offers the team a deal: kill him and go free.
- **Ends on:** Jin shakes his hand. Rei whispers, "He's lying." Yume says, "No… he isn't. That's worse."

### ARC 4: LAST ONE STANDING 最後の一人 (Episodes 16–20)
**Ep. 16 "Assault on KAGE" (影への強襲)**
- **Story:** a full military assault on the KAGE fortress under Mount Fuji. Mako's smuggler fleet shows up to make up for Ep. 8.
- **Twist:** the fortress is empty. Kurogane moved everything, and the team has walked into Saionji's trap.
- **Ends on:** the government's own soldiers surround them.

**Ep. 17 "Seventh" (七人目)**
- **Story:** a three-way battle between the team, KAGE and the government. Sora walks into the crossfire.
- **Twist:** **Sora is the seventh Gifted.** She can switch off every other Gifted's power in range.
- **Ends on:** every power goes out. It's just people with guns now.

**Ep. 18 "Morning Mist" (朝霧)**
- **Story:** Kenji touches Sora and copies her power-cancelling ability, which frees everyone. He shields the team from an artillery strike.
- **Twist:** Kenji dies to save Daigo, in his steel skin, with his memories back for his last words.
- **Ends on:** ROKU walks through the smoke and takes Kenji's white mask.

**Ep. 19 "The Original" (原型)**
- **Story:** Jin, Rei, Daigo and Yume storm Kurogane's hidden sanctuary.
- **Twist:** Kurogane is **Gifted-00, the original Sansei**. Jin and ROKU are both copies grown to replace his dying body. The Cull was a contest to choose his new body.
- **Ends on:** ROKU kneels to Kurogane, then **stabs Kurogane's prosthetic arm** instead: *"You taught me betrayal."*

**Ep. 20 "Last One Standing" (最後の一人)** *(season finale)*
- **Story:** Kurogane's final form, a 10-second-sight monster. Every Gifted uses their power at its limit together. Jin gives his eyes for one last, perfect three seconds.
- **Twist:** in the end, **Rei gives Kurogane's escape seat to Sora**, and Kurogane is left behind as the building falls. The man who wrote the Cull becomes the one it takes.
- **Ends on:** a blind Jin on a rooftop at dawn with the team. Saionji on TV announces **"Project KAGE 2."** Somewhere, ROKU puts on the white mask. *"Season 2."*

## 4. Production plan

| Item | Per episode | Whole season (20 eps) |
|---|---|---|
| Seedance 2.0 clips (15 s) | 20 | 400 |
| Credits at 1080p (135 per clip) | about 2,700 | **about 54,000** |
| Credits at 720p (67.5 per clip) | about 1,350 | **about 27,000** |
| Character or location sheets (2 each) | a few | about 60–80 total |
| Voice lines (TTS) | about 25 | about 500 |

**How to make each episode:**
1. Write the episode script using Ep. 1's format.
2. Turn it into a 20-clip plan.
3. Generate each clip with the **style lock** plus the character sheet job IDs as `image_references`.
4. Record the voices.
5. Put it together in an editor with the title card, subtitles and end card.

**Cheaper options:**
- Make episodes 1–3 at 720p to test how people respond, and upscale the ones that do well.
- Reuse transition shots (power effects, establishing shots of the fortress and Tokyo) across episodes.
- Cut 3–4 Shorts from each episode to grow the channel between releases.
